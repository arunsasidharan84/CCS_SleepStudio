# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in the CCS Sleep Studio community to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive, and assume good faith.
- Give and accept feedback graciously.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users, including clinicians and students who may be new to programming.
- Protect privacy: never post patient-identifiable data, or other people's personal information, in issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge inappropriate.
