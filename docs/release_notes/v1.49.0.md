## CCS Sleep Studio 1.49.0

Automated AASM cardiac and arrhythmia analysis (Asystole, Bradycardia, Tachycardia, PVCs, PACs, Couplets, Bigeminy/Trigeminy, VT/SVT, AFib), zoomed waveform inspection caliper and sub-second grid ticks, multi-line canonical relative band power trajectories and 2D scalp topoplots in PDF publication reports, clean single-point click markers, typable custom marker shortcuts, epoch time grid overlay, channel solo subselection mode, and decoupled 60+ FPS waveform hover performance.

### Key Highlights & Clinical Improvements

#### 1. Automated AASM Cardiac and Arrhythmia Event Analysis
- **AASM Scoring Manual Rules (Section VIII: Cardiac Rules):** Direct clinical implementation of standard ECG guidelines for sleep recordings.
- **Pan-Tompkins QRS Energy Detector:** Bandpass filtering (5–30 Hz), digital derivative squaring, moving-window integration (~150 ms), adaptive thresholding, and refractory peak lockout.
- **Automated Event Detection & Scoring:**
  - **Asystole**: Cardiac pause (R-R interval) $\ge 3.0\text{ s}$ in adults.
  - **Sinus Bradycardia**: Sustained heart rate $< 40\text{ bpm}$ ($\ge 30\text{ s}$) during sleep.
  - **Sinus Tachycardia**: Sustained heart rate $> 90\text{ bpm}$ ($\ge 30\text{ s}$) during sleep.
  - **Premature Ventricular Contractions (PVC / VPB)**: Premature wide QRS complexes ($\ge 110\text{ ms}$) with compensatory pause.
  - **Premature Atrial Contractions (PAC / APB)**: Premature narrow QRS complexes without complete compensatory pause.
  - **Ventricular Couplets & Non-Sustained Ventricular Tachycardia (NSVT)**: 2 or $\ge 3$ consecutive PVCs at rate $> 100\text{ bpm}$.
  - **Supraventricular Tachycardia (SVT)**: Run of $\ge 3$ consecutive PACs at rate $> 100\text{ bpm}$.
  - **Ventricular Bigeminy & Trigeminy**: Repetitive alternating normal and ectopic beat sequences.
  - **Atrial Fibrillation (AFib)**: Sustained irregularly irregular ventricular rhythm ($CV_{RR} \ge 0.19$, $nRMSSD \ge 0.16$).
- **Interactive Parameter Dialog:** Allows selecting the active ECG channel, customizing pause and rate thresholds, toggling arrhythmia analysis, reviewing stage-specific heart rate distributions (Wake, N1, N2, N3, REM), and committing detected events directly to the timeline.

#### 2. Zoomed Waveform Inspection with Interactive Measurement Caliper
- **Sub-Second Grid Ticks:** High-resolution time ticks (0.1s / 0.5s) and voltage grid lines in the zoomed signal dialog.
- **Interactive Caliper:** Click and drag across any section of the zoomed trace to measure:
  - Time duration $\Delta t$ in milliseconds and seconds
  - Instantaneous frequency $f = 1/\Delta t$ (Hz)
  - Amplitude difference $\Delta V$ ($\mu\text{V}$)
  - Peak-to-peak range and mean slice voltage

#### 3. Publication Sleep Report: Relative Spectral Trajectories & 2D Scalp Topoplots
- **Multi-Line Trajectory Charts:** Displays relative spectral power trajectories across canonical frequency bands (Delta, Theta, Alpha, Sigma, Beta, Gamma) across all sleep stages (Wake, N1, N2, N3, REM).
- **Side-by-Side Compact Layout:** 3 compact multi-line charts across the spectral landscape page:
  - Neural Excitability & Slope (`FOOOF exponent`, `IRASA slope`)
  - Broadband Offset & Intercept (`FOOOF offset`, `IRASA intercept`)
  - Relative Band Power Trajectory across stages (`Delta`, `Theta`, `Alpha`, `Sigma`, `Beta`, `Gamma`)
- **Robust Feature Resolution:** Resolved missing Wake state, Beta, and Gamma power values through unified alias resolution (`W_`, `Wake_`, `R_`, `REM_`, `Beta1`/`Beta2`, `Gamma1`/`Gamma2`).
- **2D Scalp Topoplots:** Automatically renders realistic head outlines, ears, nose, 10–20 electrode placements, spatial gradient interpolation, and microvolt colorbars when $\ge 5$ scalp EEG channels are present.

#### 4. Timeline Single-Point Markers & Typable Custom Shortcuts
- **Clean Pinpoint Indicators:** Suppressed compressed selection box overlays and redundant labels for single-point click markers (`startSec == endSec`).
- **Mean & Range Top Label:** Active selection overlays dynamically display both **Mean** and **Range** (Peak-to-Peak: $\Delta V = V_{\max} - V_{\min}$) in $\mu\text{V}$.
- **Keyboard Shortcuts:** Press <kbd>T</kbd> or <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>M</kbd> to open an instant prompt to enter a custom typed marker name.
- **Right-Click Context Menu:** Right-clicking directly on a selection area allows adding custom or preset markers to the selected region.

#### 5. Configurable Epoch Time Grid & Sleep Stage Watermark Ribbon
- **Vertical Time Grid:** Displays 1-second dashed and 5-second solid vertical grid lines with subtle timestamps across the epoch canvas.
- **Instant Toggle:** Toggleable on/off via the toolbar button, the View menu, or pressing <kbd>G</kbd>.
- **Stage Background Ribbon:** Subtle background tint and top-right watermark badge indicating the scored sleep stage across each epoch.

#### 6. Channel Solo / Montage Subselection Mode
- **Quick Solo:** Double-clicking any channel scale tile isolates that channel for unobstructed inspection.
- **Revert:** Double-clicking again or pressing <kbd>Esc</kbd> instantly restores the full montage.

#### 7. Waveform Hover & Scroll Performance Optimization
- **Decoupled Mouse Tracking:** Mouse hover position tracking is decoupled from full-screen `setState()` re-renders using Flutter `ValueNotifier` hooks.
- **Zero CPU Spikes:** Eliminates unnecessary viewport canvas rebuilds on cursor movements and dynamically recalculates hover amplitudes across epoch scrolling.

#### 8. Smart Clinical Decision Aid Tooltips
- **Automated Pattern Detection:** Selection windows evaluate duration, amplitude, and dominant frequency to suggest clinical candidate events:
  - Sleep Spindles ($11\text{--}16\text{ Hz}$, $0.5\text{--}3.0\text{ s}$)
  - Slow Waves / Delta ($0.5\text{--}2.0\text{ Hz}$, $>75\,\mu\text{V}$, $\ge 0.5\text{ s}$)
  - K-Complexes (biphasic, $>100\,\mu\text{V}$, $0.5\text{--}1.5\text{ s}$)
  - Alpha Rhythm ($8\text{--}12\text{ Hz}$)
  - Apnea & Hypopnea ($\ge 10\text{ s}$)
  - Oxygen Desaturations ($\ge 3\%$ drop)
  - Periodic Limb Movements (PLM) ($0.5\text{--}10\text{ s}$)
  - Phasic REM Activity

#### 9. AASM Montage EOG Derivation Standardization
- **Common Reference Consistency:** Aligned EOG derivation presets to standard common mastoid references (`LOC1-A2`, `ROC1-A2` / `E1-M2`, `E2-M2`), preventing contralateral reference mixing.

#### 10. Dynamic Display Font Scaling
- **Scalable Multiplier:** Adjustable font multiplier across all markers, labels, values, and tooltips via **View $\rightarrow$ Zoom Font In / Out / Reset** or shortcuts <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>=</kbd>, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>-</kbd>, and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>0</kbd>.
